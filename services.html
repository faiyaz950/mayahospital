Our Services

From persistent pain and spine care to IVF and women’s health — explore the treatment options and conditions listed by Maya Hospital.

Pain Management, Spine & Joint Care

Move, sleep and live with less pain.

Pain can affect mobility, everyday activities, sleep and overall quality of life. Maya Hospital lists several pain-management options and conditions for which patients may enquire about assessment and care. The appropriate approach depends on a clinician's evaluation of the individual patient.

Treatment options

Radiofrequency Treatment

Treatment using a radiofrequency machine. Ask the medical team whether this option is relevant to your diagnosis.

C-arm Guided Procedures

C-arm guided treatment relating to spinal bones and joints. Confirm the specific procedures offered and their suitability with the hospital.

PRP Therapy

Platelet-Rich Plasma (PRP) therapy is listed as a treatment option. A consultation helps you understand its potential benefits, limitations and suitability.

Physiotherapy

Contact the hospital to confirm current availability and the types of rehabilitation support offered.

IVF & Fertility Care

Support on your journey to parenthood.

IVF facilities are available at Maya Hospital with Dr. Poonam Sharma. Fertility concerns can have different causes and may require assessment of one or both partners. Individuals and couples can contact the hospital to enquire about evaluation and treatment services currently offered.

  • IVF (In Vitro Fertilization)
  • Fertility evaluation & consultation
  • Obstetrics & gynaecology consultation
  • Reproductive medicine consultation
